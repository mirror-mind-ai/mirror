# CV20.DS16.US1 — One-Turn Conditional Story Orchestration

**Type:** User Story
**Status:** ✅ Done

## Story

As Navigator, I want to naturally delegate creation and execution of the active
US/TS Plan—or choose accelerated cadence—so that Ariad can derive exact bounded
authority, preserve and complete the Driver-owned Plan, approve it only if every
structural condition still holds, start local implementation without a redundant
message, and stop for my Validation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- natural delegation does not require the Navigator to recite active-story,
  exact-scope, mismatch, or Validation policy;
- accelerated cadence records bounded story authority automatically when Plan
  begins;
- stepwise/checkpoint retain ordinary approval without natural delegation;
- vague continuation alone cannot preauthorize stepwise work;
- Plan, artifacts, approval, and implementation-start surfaces remain ordered and
  verbatim;
- execution stops at Navigator Validation;
- commit, push, tag, release, deploy, and other later gates remain blocked.
