## Readings, and what they are not

- **Every Mirror step was delivered as designed**, headless and interactive: one step
  per Mirror turn, `full` on the explicit form, `delta` on ordinary turns with the
  persona re-routed, none on a skill turn, none after a switch that landed on the
  session. Resumed processes fired no `SessionStart` and got deltas on the folder their
  persistent steps hold.
- **The model signed** every delta turn, on Gemini, headless and interactive. The
  route's reading said "unsigned" on the explicit turns because it reads the reply's
  first line, which is the Mirror Mode card; the signature follows the card.
- **Interactive row 5 answered as the mirror, and the hook was right not to deliver.**
  After the switch the session's own mode is Builder, so the doorman gave nothing; the
  model then routed itself — a garden question is a personal topic by the Operating
  Instructions — and ran `mirror mirror load` from its shell. That load cannot name the
  agy session, so the session stays Builder for the doorman, and the model loads by
  itself on each turn until the next `/mirror-mind:mm:mirror` (which re-enters, as the
  headless 4b showed). The row's expected observation ("no mirror voice") assumed no
  routing by the model; a Builder-domain question would test what it meant to.
- **The basil turn was signed `◇ demo-finance-coach`:** no keyword of the garden persona
  scored ("plant", "basil"), so the session's persona stood — the doorman's policy.
- agy's first-run Terms & data-use screen surfaced in the terminal during the headless
  phase, showing "[x] Yes, I agree to help improve Antigravity CLI by allowing Google to
  collect and use my Interactions data"; whether it was submitted, and for what scope,
  the route cannot tell.
